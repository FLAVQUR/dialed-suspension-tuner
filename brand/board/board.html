What we heard

The brief

A DIY telemetry system that records how a mountain bike's suspension works on the trail, so its rider can adjust the setup from measured data instead of feel.

precisehonesttechnicalcalm

Success: reads as a d at 16 px in a browser tab, works in one colour and reversed on the dashboard's dark page, and says 'adjusted to the right click' without drawing a bike, a mountain or a spring.

Dialed — final identity2
Final design

Dial d

The d's bowl is an adjuster dial: one lime mark across its rim, set to the click that works.

  • Ties the name to the act: 'dialed' is turning the clicker until the bike is right
  • The symbol is the first letter of the wordmark, so mark and name are one thing
  • Fog on Ink with one lime mark; in one colour the mark merges into the ring and the d stays whole
  • Lime matches none of the dashboard's data or status colours, and stays clear of Fox orange and Öhlins gold
